Uranus — old clip vs re-rendered clip

What was wrong. The voice said “Uranus” Latin/Thai-style — “oo-RAH-nuhs” (วุรานศ) — with no English y-glide and the wrong stress. The owner heard it on the live apps (2026-09-20).

What changed. Every clip below was re-rendered from the same voice, model, seed and settings, with the one difference that the renderer input respells the name — “Yoor-uh-nus” — while every word a child sees still spells “Uranus”. 28 clips from Planets & Moons (Solar System Order) and 28 from Ari & Dot (Solar Storybook).

LEFT — old clip (what plays on the live apps today)  ·  RIGHT — new clip (in this fix’s pull request, not yet published).

Listen for: the name starting with a “yoo” glide (like “Europe”) and the stress on the first syllable: YOOR-uh-nuhs. The italic line under each player is what the Mac’s own recogniser transcribed — evidence, not the verdict; your ear is the verdict. Nothing here has been published — the live apps change only after your approval of a release.
