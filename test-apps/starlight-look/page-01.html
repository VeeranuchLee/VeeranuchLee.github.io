The lights outsideWhat are all those lights?

Ari cannot sleep. The window is full of tiny lights, and tonight she knows some of them.

Ari: “I know some of those! That bright one is a planet. But the others… I don’t know what the others are at all.”

Dot: “Then tap every light in the window, and tell me which ones you know.”
