What is left behindWeight decides this too

After the explosion, something is still there in the middle. What it is depends entirely on how heavy the star was to begin with.

Careful! Not every star becomes a black hole. Almost none do. Our Sun cannot, ever — it is nowhere near heavy enough.

Stars up to about 8 Suns heavy leave a white dwarf.
A neutron star is only about 20 km wide, but heavier than the Sun.
A sugar cube of neutron star would weigh about a billion tonnes.
A black hole comes only from stars more than about 20 Suns.
Our Sun will become a white dwarf.
