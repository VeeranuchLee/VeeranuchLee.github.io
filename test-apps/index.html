Shadow Matching

Our Dictionary

The dictionary Our Word Book has never shown: 2,098 words with their Thai and a sentence each. 145 have a picture so far, so most cards read “picture to come” — that is the point of looking. Judge the reading, the search and the A–Z before more art is commissioned.

Open the dictionary → 🔊

Homograph Words

Four words are spelled the same and said differently: bow, close, tear and wind. Each now has two cards with their own recording. Search a word, then tap both speakers — the two cards must not sound alike. A staged copy of the whole dictionary; it installs nothing.

Hear the two senses → 🎹

The pinch-blocker fix for the zoom trap, on the Planets & Moons game. Zoom in with a double-tap, then PINCH OUT — you should always get back out without reloading. Badge says "staging zoom-qa".
