Write Words — no more drag

The writing paper can no longer be dragged out from under a finger: the whole writing screen locks while a word is up, the way the Writing Book always did. The grader also says why a stroke was rejected instead of only "have another go". Please write with a finger, rest a second hand on the glass, and try to make the page move.

Try to move the page → 🎨

Colouring Pilot r3 — zoom + mosaic

Same two fixes as before, at a brand-new address because the iPad kept running the old copy from Safari's cache. First thing: check the grey tag at the top-right says "staging r3" — if it does not, the page is stale. Then: Zoom — the app can no longer block pinching, so a pinch always brings the page back. Mosaic — a tap that misses an edge shape outward fills nothing instead of flooding around the card, and tiny white pockets do nothing too: open Mosaic, tap just outside a card's border and between a rocket's star points.

Try both fixes → 🪑
