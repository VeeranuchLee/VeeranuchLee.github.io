They are not neighboursThe picture only works from here

Those seven stars look like they belong together. They do not. Some are nearly twice as far away as others — they only line up because of where we happen to be standing.

Careful! A constellation is not a group of stars near each other. It is a chance line-up, seen from one place in the whole universe. Ours.
