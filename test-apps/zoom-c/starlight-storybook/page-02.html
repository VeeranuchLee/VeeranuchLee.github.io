Our Sun is a starIt only looks different because it is close

Dot flies Ari away from the Sun. Not to another planet — much further than that.

Dot: “Watch what happens to the Sun as we go. I am not making it smaller. We are just getting further away.”

Right beside EarthThe nearest star

Spreading out

Light leaves the Sun in every direction at once. Go twice as far away and the same light has to cover four times as much sky, so it looks four times fainter. Go ten times as far and it is a hundred times fainter.

That is why the Sun fades so quickly here, and why the other stars — which are just as bright as the Sun, or brighter — are only tiny points.

Distances on this page are real. The Sun’s size on screen is not to scale once it becomes a dot, or you would not be able to see it at all.
