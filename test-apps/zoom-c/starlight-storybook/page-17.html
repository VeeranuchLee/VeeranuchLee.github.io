SupernovaA star gives everything away at once

When a really heavy star runs out of fuel, its middle collapses in less than a second — and the rest of the star is thrown out across space.

Dot: “Everything the star ever made goes out into space. And later on, some of it turns into other things.”

Oxygen — the air you breathe
Calcium — your bones and teeth
Iron — the red in your blood
Gold — every ring ever made

All of it was made inside stars. Some of it is you.
