Colour is temperatureAnd it works the opposite way round to taps

A star’s colour tells you how hot it is. Slide the temperature and watch the star change.

Careful! On a tap, red means hot and blue means cold. Stars are the other way round. Blue stars are the hottest. Red stars are the coolest.

2,500° — coolest, red30,000° — hottest, blue

Are stars really these colours?

Not this strongly. Real starlight is much paler — a red star is more of a deep orange, and our Sun is very nearly white. It looks yellow from the ground because our air scatters some of the blue away.

The colours on this page are deepened on purpose, the way a star chart does it, so you can see the difference between one temperature and the next. The order is real. The strength is not.
