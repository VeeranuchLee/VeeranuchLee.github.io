A light-year is how far light gets in one whole year. It is a distance, like a kilometre — a very, very long one.

A light-year is not a year

It sounds like a length of time, and it is not. A light-year is a distance: how far light travels in one year. Nine and a half trillion kilometres.

So when we say the nearest star is 4.2 light-years away, we mean it is 4.2 light-years far. And the light you see from it left 4.2 years ago — so you are looking at what it was doing back then, not what it is doing now.
